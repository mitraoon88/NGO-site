PART A — STRATEGY, IDENTITY
& VOICE
1. Executive Summary
A2 Foundation runs Mandir Saaf Abhiyan, a voluntary Seva movement
in Ekamra Kshetra (Bhubaneswar) that (a) preserves the purity of high-
footfall temple heritage sites through daily source-segregated care, (b)
respectfully intercepts and restores sacred devotional remnants —
Pushpa, Nirmalya and terracotta diyas — diverting them from municipal
waste streams, (c) places 30–50 local women (Sevikas) at the centre of
the work as a digniﬁed, in-house, self-dependency model with direct-to-

===== PAGE 5 =====
bank livelihood remittances, and (d) funds independent evening
community learning centres giving remedial and digital education to the
Sevikas' children.
The digital platform is one WordPress installation delivering three
concentric experiences on a single database:
1. Public Sacred Narrative (Layer 1): a cinematic, editorial,
bilingual website — devotion-ﬁrst, zero commercial vocabulary —
that recruits volunteers and Sevikas, earns citizen trust, and
presents institutional-grade social proof to CSR discoverers.
2. Authenticated Operations (Layer 2): a mobile-ﬁrst work-
management portal running daily Seva collection loops, GPS
attendance, ﬁeld reporting, batch restoration tracking, remittance
registers and learning-centre administration.
3. Gated Auditor Data Room (Layer 3): lead-captured CSR
resources, full auditable ledgers, KPI schedules and prospectus
documents — the complete, unredacted institutional truth for
funders.
Design rule of the entire platform: In public, speak only of devotion,
dignity and learning. Behind the login, measure everything. In the data
room, hide nothing.

Integrity rule (non-negotiable): layering controls emphasis and
audience, never truth.
1. L3 always contains the complete, unredacted operational and
ﬁnancial reality.
2. Statutory disclosures (registration status, privacy, refund) remain
visible in the L1 footer, EN + OD.
3. Every published metric traces to an approved record; Target/
Veriﬁed badges prevent aspiration being read as fact.
4. If crafts physically generate turnover, trade-license/GST/income
obligations apply regardless of website wording — site silence is a
branding choice, never a tax strategy.
5. No false denials: copy never claims an activity doesn't exist; it
simply withholds mechanics that invite local political or tender
interference. The temple instrument remains a non-commercial
voluntary Seva MoU.

4. Editorial Voice & Public Copy Charter
4.1 Voice principles
1. Respectful and active — present tense, institutional conﬁdence
("we intercept", "we restore", "we remit").
2. Sacred terms kept intact — Seva, Nirmalya, Pushpa, Sevika,
Ekamra are never ﬂattened into generic NGO English.
3. Two reading depths per card — a bold Vision conviction line, then
plain operational detail.
4. Institutional precision wherever money or metrics are claimed;
every number attributable to a ledger record.
5. Bilingual governance: all L1 components exist in EN + OD;
machine translation of public Odia copy is prohibited; a named
native Odia editorial reviewer signs oﬀ every release.
